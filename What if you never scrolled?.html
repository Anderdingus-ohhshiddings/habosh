Methodology, assumptions, and privacy

What this includes.
Daily scrolling, an optional weekend adjustment, session frequency, bedtime minutes, current age, and life expectancy. All outputs are estimates designed to make patterns legible.

What this cannot know.
It cannot measure your actual device use, distinguish nourishing from draining content, or predict how your life will change. It does not diagnose addiction or give medical advice.

Privacy promise.
No APIs, analytics, external assets, cookies, or network requests are used. Inputs and optional history remain in this browser’s localStorage until you clear them.
